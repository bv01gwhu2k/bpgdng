What Are Peptides?

Peptides are chains of amino acids that go on to build and impact your body’s organs, tissues, and other vital systems. They are naturally found in your body and are also found in a wide variety of foods. This makes them similar to proteins, which are also chains of amino acids. However, the chains of amino acids that make peptides are smaller than those that make proteins. This causes the two to behave differently from each other.
