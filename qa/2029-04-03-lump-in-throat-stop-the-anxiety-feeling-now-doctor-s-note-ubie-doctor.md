How can anxiety contribute to a lump in the throat feeling?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Anxiety can cause the feeling of a lump in the throat, a condition known as globus sensation. This happens because stress and heightened emotional tension can tighten the muscles in the throat, creating a sensation of fullness or pressure—even when nothing is physically there. Globus sensation is common, harmless, and often resolves once anxiety is managed.
