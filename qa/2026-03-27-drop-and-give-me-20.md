Rower Lie flat on your back with your arms stretched straight behind your head and your feet together. Your hands should be extended with palms facing each other, and your head should be an inch or two off the ground. Slowly sit up, bending at the hip and knees and swinging your arms forward over your head until they are parallel with the ground and your feet are flat and tucked close to your butt. Recline and repeat. Squat Bender Begin in a standing position with your feet shoulder-distance apart and your hands on your hips. Slowly move into a squatting position while leaning your upper body forward slightly and raising your arms to be parallel to the ground, hands extended and palms facing each other. Return to the original standing position. Then, with knees slightly bent, bend over at the waste and reach for the ground, hands extended and palms facing each other. Return to a standing position to complete the exercise. Windmill Begin in a standing position with feet shoulder-distance apart and arms stretched straight out at your sides to form a T, hands extended and palms facing downward. Slowly bend at the hip and rotate your torso to the left until your right hand touches your left foot. At this point, you should be looking behind you, and your left arm should be straight behind your back. Return to an upright position and repeat for the right arm to complete the exercise. Prone Row Lie face down on the ground with your arms extended straight out and your flattened hands 1 to 2 inches off the ground. Lift your head and chest as you slowly pull your arms back (as though you are on a row machine) until your hands are almost even with your shoulders. At this point your hands should be balled into fists. Straighten out your arms and hands, and repeat. Your legs and hips should always remain in contact with the floor. Pushup Assume a pushup position with your arms straight and your hands placed slightly wider than shoulder-width apart. Your body should form a straight line from your ankles to your head. Lower your body until your chest nearly touches the floor. Keep your abdominal muscles tight to prevent your midsection from sagging. Pause, and then push back up. V-Up Lie flat on your back with your arms extended to each side so that your hands are positioned slightly below your chest, palms facing down and flush with the floor. Keep your chin tucked and your head 2 to 3 inches off the ground. Raise your legs and upper body simultaneously until you are in a V-position; use your arms for stabilization. Recline to the beginning position and repeat. Leg Tuck and Twist Begin in a sitting position with your upper body reclined as though in a lounge chair, your arms positioned slightly behind your shoulders for support, and your legs extended straight out, 8 to 12 inches off the ground. Keep your feet together and raise your legs while pulling your knees toward your left shoulder. As you do that, twist your torso so your weight shifts to the left side of your butt. Resume first position and repeat for the right shoulder to complete the exercise. Supine Bicycle Lie flat on your back with your head raised and your hands interlocked on top of your head. Your legs should be raised and bent to create a 90-degree angle with the floor. Try to touch your right elbow to your left knee; twist and tighten your core as you do. Simultaneously, extend your right leg. Return to the beginning position, and do the same with your left elbow and right knee to complete the exercise. Swimmer Lie face down with your legs and arms extended straight. Your hands should be flat and your palms and toes should be facing downward. Slowly raise your right arm and left leg 4-6 inches off the ground simultaneously. Return to the beginning position and repeat with the alternate arm and leg to complete the exercise. 8-Count Pushup Begin in a standing position with your arms at your side. Bend at the knees and squat until you are in a full crouch with chest leaned forward and your hands on the ground for support. Kick your legs backwards to assume the beginning position for a standard pushup. Perform two full pushups. From the beginning pushup position, draw your feet back into the crouching position and then stand straight up to complete the exercise.
