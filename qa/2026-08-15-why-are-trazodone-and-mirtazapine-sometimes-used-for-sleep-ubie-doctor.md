Trazodone and mirtazapine are antidepressants often prescribed off-label to help with sleep. Both medications have sedating effects that can promote drowsiness, making it easier to fall asleep and stay asleep. Trazodone is commonly used in low doses for insomnia, while mirtazapine may be prescribed when sleep issues occur alongside depression or anxiety.

Trazodone and mirtazapine are medications originally used to treat depression, but they are also sometimes used to help people sleep. This is because they have properties that can make you feel sleepy.

Why They Are Used for Sleep:

Trazodone: It helps with sleep because it can make you feel drowsy. It is often used when people have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep.

Mirtazapine: This medication also helps people feel sleepy, which can be useful for those who have insomnia, a condition where it is hard to sleep.

Both medications are considered when other sleep aids, like benzodiazepines, are not suitable. They can be helpful in managing sleep issues, especially when these issues are linked to depression. However, it is important to use them under the guidance of a healthcare provider.
