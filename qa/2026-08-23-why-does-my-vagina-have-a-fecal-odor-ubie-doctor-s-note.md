Sometimes, if you notice a fecal smell coming from your vagina, it could be a sign of a condition called a rectovaginal fistula. This is when there is a small channel or opening between your rectum and your vagina. Because of this opening, waste from the intestines can mix with vaginal secretions, giving off a fecal odor. Other times, similar smells may occur if there is inadequate hygiene or if normal bacteria in the area have changed, but a continuous fecal odor is more concerning. A rectovaginal fistula can happen due to infections, injuries during childbirth, surgeries, or other health concerns. It is important to talk with a healthcare provider if you notice a persistent fecal odor, even if there is no discharge or other symptoms. Your healthcare provider will perform tests and examinations to find out what is causing the odor and help choose a treatment plan to keep you healthy.
