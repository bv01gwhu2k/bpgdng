What ailments can be treated with an urgent care house call?

House call doctors have resources to treat more than you might think. This is in part due to the fact that most house call doctors have concierge practices, meaning they accept payment directly from patients instead of working with insurance companies. Concierge practices tend to be more patient-focused because they avoid the administrative burdens of complying with insurance company requirements and filing countless claims.

Urgent care house call providers also have access to more diagnostic tools than most people realize. House call doctors use portable equipment that allows them to process certain test results and obtain scans that can help determine the source of a patient’s symptoms. Examples of tests or screenings an urgent care house call doctor may use include:

Covid-19, Rapid Strep, and Flu tests

Lab tests to determine allergies, food sensitivities, hormone imbalances, and more

Mobile imaging scans including x-rays, ultrasounds, EKGs, or ECGs

What are the advantages of urgent care house calls over clinics?

Concierge urgent care house call providers generally have more availability over clinics that accept insurance, which may mean shorter wait times than at a clinic. Urgent care house calls are also an ideal option for households with multiple people in need of care; for example, if the whole family has influenza. Clinics may not have enough openings to accommodate an entire family on the same day. These benefits are in addition to the obvious convenience of not having to leave home when you are not feeling well.

For those at high risk of hospitalization from Covid-19, the possibility of getting infected while seeking treatment for another medical issue is scary. An urgent care house call greatly reduces the likelihood of exposure to Covid-19 and its variants you would otherwise encounter in the reception area of a clinic.
