What happens if water goes up your nose while swimming?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Water going up your nose while swimming is usually harmless, but in rare cases it can pose health risks. The main concern is exposure to Naegleria fowleri, a rare but dangerous amoeba found in warm freshwater (like lakes, hot springs, and poorly maintained pools) that can enter through the nose and cause a serious brain infection. Other possible issues include sinus irritation, bacterial infections, or minor discomfort. While these outcomes are uncommon, symptoms like severe headache, fever, stiff neck, nausea, or confusion after swimming should never be ignored.

Because symptoms from nasal water exposure can range from mild irritation to signs of a serious infection, it's important to understand what your body is telling you.
