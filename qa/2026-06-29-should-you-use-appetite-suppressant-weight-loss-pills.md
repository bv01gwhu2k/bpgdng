Are there any risks of appetite suppressants?

When properly administered and under a doctor’s supervision, appetite suppressants are extremely safe to take for certain periods. With that said, certain risks may present themselves based on your individual medical history and health.

Pulmonary hyperextension

Observational evidence suggests that appetite suppressants may increase the likelihood of developing this condition, which affects the right side of the heart and the lungs’ arteries5. Symptoms may include chronic shortness of breath, dizziness, and chest pain that can increase in intensity over time.

Valvular heart disease

Defined as a condition in which the heart’s valve is damaged or diseased. While there appears to be a link between valvular heart disease and products like appetite suppressants, there is conflicting data on just how much of a risk exists6.

Cardiac-valve regurgitation

Sometimes referred to as a “leaky heart valve,” this is a condition in which a heart valve cannot close completely, causing a “regurgitation” effect. Although studies suggest a correlation between this condition and appetite suppressants, the numbers tend to be low7.
