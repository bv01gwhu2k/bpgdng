Cetirizine and fexofenadine are second-generation antihistamines that may help reduce nasal congestion linked to sleep apnea. Because allergic rhinitis is a known contributor to disrupted sleep and worsened apnea symptoms, treating the underlying allergy can improve airflow and sleep quality.

However, nasal congestion is only one of many possible factors behind sleep apnea, and self-treating without understanding the root cause can delay proper care. Explanation

Nasal congestion from allergic rhinitis can make sleep apnea worse. Cetirizine and fexofenadine are antihistamines that help reduce allergy symptoms like nasal congestion. According to the provided references, allergic rhinitis is closely linked to sleep issues, including sleep apnea.

Allergic Rhinitis and Sleep Apnea: Allergic rhinitis can lead to blocked nasal passages, which might worsen sleep apnea. This condition can cause poor sleep quality and daytime tiredness.

Role of Antihistamines: Cetirizine and fexofenadine are medications that can relieve nasal congestion by reducing the body's allergic response. This might help improve breathing during sleep.
