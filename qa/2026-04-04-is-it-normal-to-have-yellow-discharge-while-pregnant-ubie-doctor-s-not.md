During pregnancy, it is common for your body to produce more vaginal discharge because of hormonal changes and increased blood flow. This extra fluid helps keep the area clean and healthy. Normally, the discharge is clear or white. However, sometimes the color can shift slightly, and you might notice a yellow tint. In some cases, a light yellow discharge can be normal. But if the yellow discharge is thick, has a strong odor, or is accompanied by symptoms like itching, burning, or irritation, it could indicate an infection. Infections such as bacterial vaginosis or even other issues could cause the discharge to change in color and consistency during pregnancy. Because pregnancy involves additional considerations, it’s always a good idea to mention any noticeable changes in your vaginal discharge to your healthcare provider. They can perform a simple exam and any necessary tests to determine if the yellow discharge is just a normal change or if it requires treatment. Keeping track of these changes can help ensure that both you and your baby remain healthy.
