Explanation

Burping a lot, also known as excessive belching, can occur if you swallow too much air. This is called aerophagia. People might swallow more air when they are anxious or stressed. Sometimes, burping too much can be a sign of a digestive problem, but it's often not serious.

Causes of Excessive Burping

Swallowing Air (Aerophagia): People swallow air when they eat or drink too fast, chew gum, or smoke.

Stress or Anxiety: Feeling stressed or anxious can cause some people to swallow more air.

Digestive Issues: Sometimes, burping too much is linked to digestive problems like acid reflux.
