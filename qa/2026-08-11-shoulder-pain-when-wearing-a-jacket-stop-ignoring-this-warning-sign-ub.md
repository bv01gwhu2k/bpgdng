Why do I experience shoulder pain when wearing a jacket?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Wearing a jacket can cause shoulder pain when tight or heavy clothing restricts natural movement of the spine and shoulders. This added pressure and limited range of motion can strain muscles, compress nerves, and lead to discomfort or stiffness—especially if you already have underlying tension, poor posture, or a shoulder condition.
