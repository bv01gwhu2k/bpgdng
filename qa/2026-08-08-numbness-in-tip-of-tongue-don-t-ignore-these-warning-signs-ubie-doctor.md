Numbness in the tip of the tongue can happen for a few different reasons. Sometimes, it is due to nerve injuries. For example, medical procedures like using certain devices during surgery can accidentally harm the nerves in the tongue, leading to numbness. This is discussed in the research by Wang and colleagues.

Another possible reason for tongue numbness is related to the brain. Chen and colleagues explain that changes in the brain, such as those affecting one side of the brain, can cause isolated numbness in the tongue. This can happen without other symptoms.

Lastly, Shimohata and colleagues mention that numbness in the tongue and other areas like the lip can be linked to brain conditions, like a stroke. These conditions affect how signals are sent from the brain to different parts of the body, leading to numbness.
