Injury or trauma: Sprains, strains, bruises, or fractures can cause swelling. Infections: Skin infections like cellulitis or abscesses can cause redness, warmth, and swelling. Allergic reactions: Bites, stings, or contact with allergens can cause localized swelling. Blood clots (deep vein thrombosis): A clot in a vein may block blood flow and cause swelling. Lymphedema: Blockage or damage to lymph vessels, often related to surgery or radiation, can cause fluid buildup. Venous obstruction: Compression or blockage of veins can lead to swelling.
