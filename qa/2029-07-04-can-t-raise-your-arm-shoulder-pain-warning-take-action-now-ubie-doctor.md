Difficulty raising your arm due to shoulder pain can be caused by several underlying conditions, including frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), rotator cuff injuries, or tendonitis. Risk factors include diabetes, thyroid disorders, and previous treatments such as shoulder surgery or radiotherapy, which can lead to stiffness and restricted movement. Because shoulder pain has many possible causes—ranging from mild strain to more serious joint or nerve issues—a proper medical assessment is essential to identify the root cause and guide treatment.
