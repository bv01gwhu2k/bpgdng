Why is my bladder itchy, and what can I do about it?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Bladder itchiness is often caused by irritation, infection, or inflammation of the urinary tract or surrounding tissues. Common triggers include urinary tract infections (UTIs), yeast infections, harsh soaps, or allergic reactions. To find relief, keep the genital area clean and dry, avoid scented hygiene products, wear breathable cotton underwear, and stay well-hydrated. If itching persists, worsens, or is accompanied by burning, discharge, or pain, consult a doctor promptly.
