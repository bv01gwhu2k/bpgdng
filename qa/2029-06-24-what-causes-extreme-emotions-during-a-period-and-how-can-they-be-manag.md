What causes extreme emotions during a period and how can they be managed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Extreme emotional symptoms during your period—such as intense mood swings, irritability, sadness, or anxiety—are often caused by hormonal fluctuations in estrogen and progesterone. For many people, these symptoms can be managed with lifestyle adjustments like regular exercise, balanced nutrition, quality sleep, stress reduction, and social support. However, severe emotional distress may indicate a condition like PMDD (Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder) and should be evaluated by a mental health professional.
