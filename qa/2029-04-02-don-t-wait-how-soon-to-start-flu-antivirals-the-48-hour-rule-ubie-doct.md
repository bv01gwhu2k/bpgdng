Published on: 7/21/2026

How soon should you start taking antiviral medicine after flu symptoms begin?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Antiviral medicine works best when started within 48 hours of the first flu symptoms. Taken early, antivirals can shorten the duration of illness, reduce symptom severity, and lower the risk of complications like pneumonia. Waiting longer than two days significantly reduces their effectiveness, so acting quickly is essential.
