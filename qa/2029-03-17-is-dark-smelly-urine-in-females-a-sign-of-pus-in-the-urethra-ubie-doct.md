Key Points:

Smell and Color: While smelly urine can be a sign of a UTI, the color and smell alone do not confirm the presence of pus in the urethra.

Other Symptoms: UTIs may also cause symptoms like a burning sensation when urinating, frequent urination, and sometimes fever.

Diagnosis: To determine if there is an infection or pus, a healthcare provider may perform tests such as a urinalysis.

If you notice dark, smelly urine along with other symptoms, it's important to speak with a healthcare provider for proper evaluation and treatment.
