What is Ketamine, and how do veterinarians use it for pain relief?

Ketamine is described as a dissociative anesthetic. It has been the subject of groundbreaking research in humans and has been a mainstay in veterinary medicine for several years.

It has primarily been used as a sedative when treating animals (mainly dogs and cats) at the veterinary clinic. 2

The medication works by interfering with how the central nervous system processes pain. One of the reasons veterinarians use it so frequently is because it does not need to be administered via IV to take effect on the animal; it can be injected directly into the muscle, making it much easier to administer than other sedative medications or anesthesia.

When used in pain relief, it relaxes muscles and induces sleepiness, which helps control chronic pain in animals. The medicine serves a dual purpose, easing the animal’s pain and providing a calming effect. However, due to the dissociative effect, the medication must be used in a very controlled manner.
