# The Only Way to Wear a Graphic Tee

The graphic tee is a men’s style staple.

[Way to Wear](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Here’s how to rock the look so you appear more stylish, and less like a devoted Phish fan in the ’90s.

Pick the Right Graphic Tee For You

[Wear Graphic](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/)

[to Wear Graphic Tee](https://peplawllc.com/)

Let your shirt do the talking. Make your shirt the focal point of your outfit, and keep everything else subdued and simple.

[Wear Graphic](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

[to Wear](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/5200980941.html)

You can go with a classic, like a band tee, or you can go with a tribute to The Greatest, Muhammad Ali. ($24, urbanoutfitters.com).

But don’t feel as if you need to make a bold statement with your graphic shirt. Minimalist shirts can speak volumes, too. ($125, bloomingdales.com).

Related: How to Overhaul Your Wardrobe on a Budget

Pair Your Graphic Tee With Pants

Skip the cargo shorts. (And while you’re at it, skip the sandals, too.)

To keep the look modern, pair your tee with a cool pair of slim-fitting denim, like blogger Everett Williams (pictured above). Here’s a similar pair we like from AG Jeans ($178, ag.com).

[Only Way](https://benpepsi.com/)

Cuff the bottom of the jeans to expose a pair of low-top sneakers—bonus points for a pair with a cool print, like these from Vans ($65, vans.com). As for socks, opt for either high-tops or no-shows.

Related: The Best Thing to Wear Every Day of the Week

Perfect the Other Details

Keep accessories to a minimum so you can keep attention on your killer shirt. All you need is a watch or a pair of sunglasses.

[Way to Wear Graphic](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/3784938611.html)

[The Only Way to](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/2762887942.html)

Before you head out the door, tuck in just the front of your shirt like Williams, and let the rest hang loose.
