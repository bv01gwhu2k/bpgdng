# Wiz Khalifa Reveals the Only Part of His Body That Isn’t Tatted

[Wiz Khalifa Reveals](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/)

When Men's Health Hip Hop 50 cover star Wiz Khalifa was 21 years old, he proclaimed in his song "Ink My Whole Body" that you may not have taken as a life goal. On the song, he staunchly declared, "I'ma ink my whole body, I don't give a motherfuck." If you've seen his canvas of a chest, decorated arms, and slightly inked face, you know he's made good on his promise. But he's not done, as he explained to Men's Health in the latest episode of The Rewind. "I've tattooed all of the fun spots. The only places I have left are my ass cheeks. So, whenever I decide to do that, we'll get some martial arts tattoos."

That may or may not be an implicit admission that other private areas have been adorned with works of art, but what is clear in this episode is the 36-year-old father has grown into a healthier version of his past self. He still consumes enough cannabis to knock out a small ox, but he's more aware of the importance of body maintenance. He has a personal chef come with him on the road in order for him to get balanced meals, and he now works out daily compared to the non-existent workout routine he had when he was in his early 20s. He admits that while he never prioritized his mental health when he was stage diving at shows and building his brand to where it is now, fatherhood has given him a much-needed outlet for his stress.
