Sleep apnea in men is a common sleep disorder marked by three key symptoms: loud, chronic snoring, episodes of gasping or choking for air during sleep, and excessive daytime sleepiness despite a full night's rest. Other signs may include morning headaches, difficulty concentrating, and irritability. Left untreated, sleep apnea can increase the risk of high blood pressure, heart disease, and stroke. Because symptoms often overlap with other conditions, identifying the root cause early is critical. Sleep apnea in men can present with several noticeable symptoms. The most common ones include loud snoring, which often gets noticed by others, and gasping or choking sounds during sleep. These symptoms are caused by the airway getting blocked during sleep. Because of this, men with sleep apnea might also feel very tired during the day, even if they think they slept enough at night.

Key Symptoms:

Loud Snoring: This is often the most noticeable symptom and can be disruptive to others.

Gasping or Choking: Men might wake up suddenly feeling like they can't breathe.

Daytime Sleepiness: Feeling very sleepy or tired during the day, which can affect daily activities.

Sleep apnea can affect how well you sleep and how you feel during the day. If you notice these symptoms, you can take a free symptom checker test to better understand what might be causing them, and it can help guide your conversation with a doctor about what's happening.
