Excessive daytime sleepiness in older adults can be caused by various factors including some medications and/or medical conditions.

It is important to seek help from a healthcare provider to identify what is causing excessive daytime sleepiness. Here are some examples of factors that can cause excessive daytime sleepiness in older people:

Some Medications: Some medicines, like certain pain medications, can make people sleepy during the day. Seek advice from a healthcare provider before making any adjustments to medications.

Some Medical Conditions: A wide variety of health conditions that affect older people can impair sleep and cause excessive daytime sleepiness. For example, obstructive sleep apnea is most common among older men (but can also affect women and younger people). Another example is chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, which can affect older people and also contribute to daytime sleepiness. Depression is another medical condition that can affect sleepiness during the day.

Menopause: Menopause usually affects older women and menopause symptoms can affect sleep. To better understand your symptoms and potential causes, you can use Ubie's free AI symptom checker.
