# This Challenging Kettlebell Flow Workout Is Ideal for Beginners

Eric Leija is the master of the kettlebell flow routine, so if you're interested in trying out this kind of full body workout for the first time, there's no better person to demonstrate some simple beginner moves.

"If you really focus on owning every position and maximize intention, you can take these foundational movements to really get you worked," says Leija in a recent video which outlines a five-move flow routine. He adds that this can easily be done as a workout; simply perform 10 sets, 40 seconds on with 20 seconds of rest.

Leija starts with the deadlift, ensuring his shoulders are pulled back and down and his core is tight before hinging at the waist to grip the bell and explosively pull it up. He then lowers the bell back to the ground and starts the second move: a kettlebell row.

"I want you to own the movement here by squeezing your back, your lats, as you row that elbow tight to your ribs, back towards your head," he says.

Once the bell is back on the ground, he uses his opposite hand to assist in spiraling the handle of the bell around his wrist in a cheat clean. From there, Leija goes straight into a squat, bending his knees forward and keeping his spine strong, and pausing at the top end of the movement before lifting it into a kettlebell press.
