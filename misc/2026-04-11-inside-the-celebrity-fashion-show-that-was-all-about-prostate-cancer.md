# Inside the Celebrity Fashion Show That Was All About Prostate Cancer

This New York Fashion Week, one show broke the mold by raising awareness and funding for prostate cancer research.

[All About Prostate Cancer](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/7771355627.html)

[All About Prostate](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/2238954112.html)

On February 7th, The 3rd Annual The Blue Jacket Fashion show (sponsored by the Janssen Pharmaceutical Companies of Johnson & Johnson) featured famous actors, athletes, and fashion insiders such as Drake Bell, Buster Skrine, Billy Porter, and Ryan Eggold putting their best foot forward on the catwalk, donning blue designer jackets. Even The Real Housewives of New York City joined the band of notable men on the runway. Why? Because prostate cancer is the most common non-skin cancer in America and nearly 165,000 men were diagnosed with prostate cancer in 2018 alone. As official media sponsors of the event, Men’s Health was honored to be a part of this event.

[Inside the Celebrity Fashion](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/4647077299.html)

Iconic fashion labels like Tommy Hilfiger, Hugo Boss, Michael Kors, Emporio Armani, and more, dressed the “models” in looks that ran the full gamut of a modern man’s wardrobe—polished athleisure, elevated denim ensembles, casual-cool suiting, and even a plush electric blue tuxedo received roaring applause from the crowd. It was a show you wouldn’t want to miss, but if you did, here’s a glimpse at some of our favorite looks to inspire some stylish outfit ideas and hopefully, inspire you to get involved in this worthy cause.
