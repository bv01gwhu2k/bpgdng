How is myasthenia gravis defined in medical literature?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Myasthenia gravis is an autoimmune disorder that causes muscle weakness. It occurs when the immune system mistakenly attacks the connections between nerves and muscles, disrupting communication and leading to fatigue, drooping eyelids, difficulty swallowing, and weakness that worsens with activity.
