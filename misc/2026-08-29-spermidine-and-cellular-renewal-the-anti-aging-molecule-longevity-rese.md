How Spermidine Supports Cellular Renewal

Cellular renewal is the process by which old, damaged cell components are broken down and replaced by new ones. Spermidine appears to enhance this process primarily through autophagy:

Autophagy Activation

Autophagy is a natural mechanism cells use to remove worn-out proteins and organelles.

Spermidine has been shown to induce autophagy, helping cells stay "clean" and function optimally.

Safety and Side Effects

Current research indicates spermidine is generally well tolerated, but it's important to be aware of potential issues:

Gastrointestinal Upset: Mild symptoms like gas or bloating may occur.

Allergies: Avoid supplements derived from wheat if you have celiac disease or wheat sensitivity.

Medication Interactions: If you're on anticoagulants, immunosuppressants, or other chronic medications, check with your doctor.

Overall, adverse effects are rare at recommended doses. Long-term human safety data are still being collected.
