Everything you need to know about PRP Hair Restoration

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Hair Restoration is a natural treatment that uses the patient’s own blood to stimulate hair growth. It involves extracting PRP from the blood and injecting it into the scalp to promote hair follicle regeneration and hair growth.

PRP Hair Restoration can potentially restore hair growth, promote blood flow to the scalp, and help prevent the need for hair transplant surgery. It is also a relatively affordable treatment with minimal side effects.

PRP is generally safe, with minimal side effects since it uses the patient’s own blood. Some individuals may experience minor swelling, redness, or discomfort at the injection sites.

The procedure may cause mild discomfort during the injections, but most patients tolerate it well. A topical anesthetic may be applied to the scalp to minimize any discomfort.

PRP can help slow down or reverse hair loss in some cases, but it may not completely prevent baldness, especially if the hair loss is due to genetic factors. However, it is a helpful tool in a comprehensive hair loss treatment plan.

Men’s hormone therapy, especially testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), can be combined with PRP hair restoration treatments depending on individual needs and goals.

TRT addresses hormonal imbalances, which can be a cause of hair loss, while PRP boosts hair growth by stimulating hair follicles with platelets and growth factors.

Together, they can provide complementary benefits: TRT improves overall health and tackles the root cause of hair loss, while PRP regenerates follicles for hair growth.

The therapy involves drawing a small amount of the patient’s blood, processing it to concentrate the platelets and growth factors, and then injecting the PRP back into areas of the scalp where hair growth is desired. These growth factors help stimulate the hair follicles and promote blood flow.

PRP Hair Restoration is ideal for individuals experiencing hair loss or thinning due to genetic factors, hormonal imbalances, poor nutrient status, or stress. Both men and women can benefit from the treatment.

The number of treatments varies based on the individual’s hair loss condition and response to the therapy. Typically, patients receive 3 to 4 treatments spaced about 4 to 6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance treatments every few months.

Results vary from person to person, but many patients start to see improvements in hair growth and thickness within 3 to 6 months after their initial treatments. Full results may take up to a year.

PRP Hair Restoration is not a permanent cure for hair loss, but it can significantly improve hair growth and thickness when combined with other treatments and regular maintenance sessions.
