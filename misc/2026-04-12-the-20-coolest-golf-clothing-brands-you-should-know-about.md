Lululemon

Last year Lululemon, one of our favorite athleisure brands, finally gave us the golf collection we've been waiting for, and we haven't looked back since. Imagine your best Lululemon garments, but with a touch more room and a more friendly cut that allows you to take powerful hacks without ever worrying about your clothes inhibiting your swing. Sounds pretty great, right?

After testing a year's worth of Lululemon's golf shirts and pants, we found that they also look great off the course, so you can get away with wearing a simple outfit to work and then go straight to the course for an evening round.

Wilson

Another legacy golf brand on this list, Wilson has put out an impressively large collection of apparel with modern styling and elite performance. The brand has knockout garments across its entire board, from golf pants that can be worn right off the course to outerwear with excellent wind protection. Pick up something versatile like the Tour Shacket, a home run piece we tested when playing in cool temperatures between 55 and 70-degrees. Or you could go with one of their retro-inspired sweatshirts that are reminiscent of what your dad might have worn years ago.

RLX Golf

Ralph Lauren's RLX Golf line is filled with sporty essentials that are clean and appropriate for even the most exclusive country clubs. The clothes are cut with performance fabrics and come in a looser fit, allowing you to move freely through your swing. RLX Golf pieces are also timeless, so you never have to worry about different design and patterns falling out of taste.

Simply put, if you're looking for a through-line through that goes from an elevated wardrobe to your golf attire, you can't go wrong adding some RLX Golf to wear outside of your best Ralph.

Vuori Clothing Vuori Clothing Meta Jogger

Vuori doesn't necessarily have a ton of dedicated golf garments, but our testing has shown the clothes wear just as good on the links as they do off. We've played all day in Vuori's Ace Polo many times, and we've taken the Meta Pant for a spin too. However the most unique Vuori golf piece we liked is the Meta Jogger. It's perfect for wearing to the driving range to practice your game.

Adidas

Most of the golf brands on this list put out pretty solid rain gear, but the adidas Rain.Rdy jackets combine the best water resistant tech in a comfortable, stylish look. You'll pay up a bit for the rain jacket, but when you consider how long its going to last the cost per wear will definitely impress you over time.

All of adidas golf in general is premium quality too, so if you pickup the rain jacket it's worth matching it up with a polo and a pair of pants. Long live the three stripes.

Peter Millar

A longtime favorite for serious golfers, Peter Millar is all about clean, performance-ready pieces. The garments are always easy to match and timeless in style, so the high price point can be viewed as an upfront investment that will factor out quite well when you figure the overall cost per wear.

We like the quality of the brand's hybrid pieces, as they offer easy solutions to those days when temperatures fluctuate and the weather conditions are slightly unpredictable.

Puma

Puma has garnered high attention for its modern digs over the years, and while the brand has tons of hidden gems in its golf apparel (and golf shoes) the most iconic piece has got to be the brand's signature "P" golf hats. Puma continues to remix the popular hat in tons of different styles and colors, making it easy to find a dome piece that's full of expression and made with a cut that provides proper shade.

Under Armour

Under Armour has been churning out top-of-the-line golf gear for years now while sponsoring some of the biggest names in the game. You can't go wrong with any of UA's golf pieces—their golf belts are probably our favorite among all of the brands—but if you're building a comprehensive golf wardrobe you'll definitely want to diversify your polo shirts. Go for one of UA's striped or graphic polos. It will last for quite a long time.

Bonobos

Bonobos' golf attire carries the same qualities as its comfortable clothes: ample stretch and a premium moisture-wicking ability. There are a few different types of pieces you can grab from the brand, but really where Bonobos shines on the course is in its golf shorts.

Devereux

Following a mantra that reads "Play More, Complain Less", Devereux provides fresh and bold designs to the golf space that pushes the boundaries just enough without going overboard. The Texas brand has been on a roll with its collection drops in the past year. Our favorite garment category from the brand right now are its polos. You simply can't get these styles anywhere else.

TRUE Linkswear

TRUE Linkswear has a casual, West Coast energy you can feel in all its apparel offerings. Everything from its pants to tees promise a comfortable feel, but the one area the brand does exceptionally well is its footwear. The All Day Knit series is one of our favorite golf shoes to date thanks to its breathable mesh and longstanding materials that hold up great on and off course. We just re-upped on the new All Day Knit 3.

Brady Brand

Brady Brand has simple, performance-driven pieces that will match with nearly all the golf attire you have in your wardrobe already. You can go a couple of routes when it comes to shopping Brady Brand, but we highly recommend its lightweight vest. The staple piece will be a mainstay in your rotation for years to come.

Eastside Golf

Golf has grown significantly in diversity over the past few years, and brands like Eastside Golf are some of the core reasons why that trend continues to grow. The brand's core beliefs are to raise awareness to "youth and non golfers" and to promote diversity to the game while staying authentic.

Eastside Golf has rallied off a number of fresh collaborations, including ones with Jordan Brand and the NBA. This is a golf apparel brand that's changing the game in real time.

Malbon Golf

Malbon Golf has been around for over half a decade now, but the streetwear-inspired brand caught fire during the golf boom that occurred during the Pandemic. Malbon Golf has kept its momentum going ever since, churning out stylish and sporty collaborations with big name brands like Nike, adidas, New Balance, and more.

The range and creativity of Malbon Golf seems to have no bounds, making it a prime brand to shop if you want the coolest new apparel the sport has to offer. The only downside about buying this brand is the demand is high, so sell-outs are common.

Chubbies

Chubbies golf apparel line hasn't been out as long as some of these other brands, but we've found the quality of the clothing is some of the best when it comes to playing in hot temperatures. The brand has some insanely lightweight and breathable polo styles (with unique patterns, no less). But our top garment pick is their stretchy, above-the-knee cut shorts. Mizzen + Main

Phil Mickelson might not be an official sponsor for Mizzen + Main anymore, but he's a huge reason for how the brand become so well known for its golf apparel. We're talking pieces that are big on comfort and timeless in style.

Bogey Boys

Founded by GRAMMY-award winning artist and avid golfer Macklemore, Bogey Boys blends the best of old-school golf style with modern streetwear. The result is a golf brand that plays with multiple silhouettes and provides fresh designs that will have you standing out in a crowd.
