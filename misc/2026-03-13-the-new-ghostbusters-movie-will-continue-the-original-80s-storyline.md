# The New 'Ghostbusters' Movie Will Continue the Original '80s Storyline

When you need a new Ghostbusters sequel, who you gonna call? Jason Reitman, son of the director of the 1984 classic!

According to Entertainment Weekly, Reitman — son of Ivan Reitman, who directed the original Ghostbusters — has signed on to co-write and direct a new installment of the beloved horror-comedy set in the original Ghostbusters universe.

“I’ve always thought of myself as the first Ghostbusters fan, when I was a six-year-old visiting the set,” Reitman said in an exclusive interview published Wednesday. “I wanted to make a movie for all the other fans.”

The award-winning film — which found Bill Murray, Dan Aykroyd, and Harold Ramis running a kind of pest-extermination business aimed at eliminating ghosts — has already spawned a 1989 sequel and a 2016 remake helmed by breakout performance by Kate McKinnon. But, the younger Reitman told EW, this latest Ghostbusters flick won’t be a relaunch, but a continuation of the original storyline.

“This is the next chapter in the original franchise,” the Juno and Up in the Air director continued. “It is not a reboot. What happened in the '80s happened in the '80s, and this is set in the present day.”

It’s not yet clear who will star in the new film, which will be produced by the elder Reitman, or if Aykroyd, Murray, or any other of the original stars will return. (Ramis died in 2014.) There’s also no word yet on the plot, but the torch passing is slated for release in 2020.
