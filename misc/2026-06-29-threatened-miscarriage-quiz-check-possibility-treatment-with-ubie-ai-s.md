What is Threatened Miscarriage?

A threatened abortion or miscarriage are the same condition. A patient may present with vaginal bleeding or cramping in early pregnancy, but an ultrasound will show a live fetus (baby) and the patients cervix will be closed. The majority of these patients will continue to have a normal pregnancy, but some will progress to complete miscarriage.

Typical Symptoms of Threatened Miscarriage

Currently pregnant

Feel like water has broken/amniotic fluid is coming out

Vaginal bleeding or discolored discharge outside of your period

Stomach or abdominal pain accompanied by a hard abdomen

Increased vaginal discharge that is smelly

History of preterm labor

Fever

Feeling contractions very frequently

Diagnostic Questions for Threatened Miscarriage

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you pregnant?

Do you feel like your water has broken?

Have you experienced any vaginal bleeding or unusual discharge outside of your period?

Are you experiencing stomach or abdominal pain with a hard abdomen?

Has the vaginal discharge odor worsened recently?
