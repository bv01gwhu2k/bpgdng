What Will You Learn on an Online Dermal Filler Course?

Online dermal filler courses cover a wide range of topics and techniques, ensuring that you’re well-equipped to perform these procedures safely and effectively. Here's what you can expect to learn:

1. Types of Dermal Fillers and Their Uses

The first thing you'll learn is the different types of dermal fillers available, such as hyaluronic acid, calcium hydroxylapatite, and poly-L-lactic acid. Each type of filler has unique properties and is used for specific facial areas. You will explore:

Hyaluronic Acid Fillers: Most commonly used for softening wrinkles, plumping lips, and restoring facial volume.

Calcium Hydroxylapatite Fillers: Used for deeper wrinkles and facial contouring.

Poly-L-lactic Acid: Known for stimulating collagen production, helping with skin texture and volume restoration.

You will also understand how to select the right filler based on the treatment area and the patient's skin type.

2. Facial Anatomy and Injection Techniques

In order to perform dermal filler injections safely, a deep understanding of facial anatomy is essential. This online masterclass offers in-depth lessons on facial structures such as:
