Air Jordan 1 Mid

SHOP AIR JORDAN 1 LOW

The Air Jordan that started it all, and is inarguably the most iconic Jordan of all time—period. Introduced in 1985, the AJ1 marked the beginning of the collaboration between Nike and the young, rising star in the NBA. In an era when most basketball shoes looked conservative and muted, the Air Jordan I came into the scene looking all flamboyant with a high-top silhouette and bold colorways. But what truly set the AJ1 apart was its tech innovation: the now iconic Air-Sole cushioning unit in the midsole which offers game-changing comfort and support for athletes. Nike’s latest remix on AJ1 maintains the iconic look that ballers and sneakerheads everywhere will recognize, but it is updated with modern materials to riff on the 1985 on-court sensation. For instance, the upper is made with leather and textile to feel more supportive, the toe-box is perforated to let your feet breathe, and the rubber outsole now has a basketball-specific traction pattern to boost grip on the court's floor. But don’t forget the time-tested performance of the midsole: the lightweight Nike Air cushioning provides support and impact absorption. Although the new colorways are nowhere near as iconic or sought-after as the "Bred" and "Chicago", the MJ spirits are still there for sure in these new tones.

Another fun fact about the AJ1 is that Michael Jordan was fined $5,000 each time he wore the model on the court because of the NBA's strict uniform policy. That, of course, only fueled the popularity and cult status of the sneaker. That is not to say the Air Jordan 1 is all style and no substance—as previously established, the cushioning in the midsole meets the athletic standards. But that’s not enough, even in the redo of AJ1 above. Hence, Jordan introduced the CMFT model—short for, obviously, “comfort”—which is a thorough upgrade that’ll really make you want to wear it every day.

Overall, the Air Jordan 1 Zoom CMFT 2 feels comfortable even for long hours on your feet, and the upper and toe are both made in suede to be able to break in easily while conforming to the shape of your feet. No doubt the wearability is improved. But the performance is just as robust if not more: the Air Jordan 1 Zoom CMFT 2 still has the Formula 23 foam for added underfoot cushioning, and the Zoom Air unit in the midsole absorbs impact while providing energy return.

Overall, the fit feels extra cozy and lets wearers score 100% on both comfort and performance on or off the court.

Air Jordan 3 Retro

Next to the Air Jordan 1, the AJ3 is perhaps the second most iconic: released in 1988, it is the first model of the line to be designed by Nike’s legendary designer, Tinker Hatfield, and it is the first Air Jordan to don the Jumpman logo. As for the model’s court history, MJ in fact was considering leaving Nike, but after seeing Hatfield’s design, he knew he’d better stay and make history with the AJ3. Later, MJ won his first MVP award in these Jordans, and the rest is history.

Really, the AJ3 was like nothing anyone had seen before on the court when MJ wore it in 1988, and its future as a streetwear icon was surely secured as soon as it was released. This updated version is both a throwback and an upgrade. It has all the classic style motives of the OG AJ3: elephant-print panels at the toe and heel, Jumpman logo branding, a visible Air unit in the heel, and TPU lace eyelets. But the performance is spiced up now with a plush foam midsole that provides stable cushioning and ankle support, an Air-Sole unit in the forefoot for energy return, and a circular traction pattern in the rubber outsole for enhanced traction.

Look-wise, this is truly the best retro Jordan style. Made with a leather upper in the wild elephant print, it’s easy to style and easy to stand out.

For support, these Jordans feel explosive with the Zoom Air unit that provides nice bouncy cushioning. For stability, the model has a midfoot plate that runs through the shoe to keep wearers surefooted, an interior bootie that makes sure the shoes never slip off as you are in action, and a herringbone outsole that provides traction to every step.

For speed, the energy-returning midsole helps players generate bursts of velocity, and the lightweight upper feels airy but still sturdy to never slow you down; more importantly, the rubber outsole is wrapped nearly up to the midsole to ensure stability when your speed goes from 0 to 100. Looking as good as it performs too, the Why Not .6 lets you stand out on the court, do quick bursts of speed, as well as stop on a dime with ease.

Jordan ADG 4

MJ himself admits to playing 36 holes of golf nearly every day, so it’s safe to say that he’s probably as fantastic on the basketball court as he is on the golf course. Hence, a pair of Jordan golf shoes is worth considering if you’re also a weekend warrior. In fact, the Jordan ADG 4 is described by Nike as “a request from MJ himself,” wherein retro Jordan flair meets the classic golf shoe look. The leather upper is soft, supple, and easy to break in but still sturdy and stable enough to ground your feet as you swing. Same with the rubber sole which has an integrated traction pattern that gives you the high-functioning grip and traction you need on—and off—the course, on a variety of surfaces.

Moreover, these Jordan golf shoes are lightweight with heavy-duty cushioning from the midsole’s foam, known as Nike React™, that not only turns every step into spring energy return but also offers support.

The OG model sports a clean-cut design, a unique lacing system, a leather mudguard, and a translucent outsole. This modern upgrade now puts that familiar profile on top of Zoom Air's responsive cushioning and lightweight foam for all-day-long, every-step comfort. In fact, the collar and tongue are both padded to make you want to wear the new AJ11 24/7. With an upper that now comes with perforations throughout for breathability, your feet will stay cool and dry in these kicks too.

Additionally, we love how the fabric wraps the top of the foot for more stability, and how the rubber outsole provides even more traction and durability in areas where they’re needed the most. These features come together to make the AJ11 a great pair of walking shoes, for slow strolls or running for your life.

Jordan Jumpman MVP

Created to celebrate MJ's legendary first 3-peat championship run, the Jumpman MVP took design elements from the Air Jordan 6, 7, and 8 while looking completely one of a kind with leather, textile, and nubuck details on the upper for a fuller, more textured look, almost beckoning wearers to do the same: honoring a legacy while building your own.

To be specific, the tongue top and the outsole design reference the AJ6 while the boot-like lining pays homage to the AJ7. As for the molded heel detail, that comes from the AJ8. On its own, the Jumpman MVP has the Nike Air-Sole unit that provides lightweight cushioning, a lace-up closure that provides a secure fit, and a heel loop that makes getting it on and off easier. It’s not as performance-driven as the models it took inspiration from, but still nonetheless an excellent pair of Jordans if you wear them as lifestyle, casual everyday sneakers.

Jordan Jumpman Pro Quick

First released in 1997, Jordan’s Jumpman Pro is a model that deserves some more love and recognition: it holds a pivotal role in the chronicles of Air Jordan sneakers as the inaugural release under the newly established Jordan Brand. This momentous step came as a result of Michael Jordan and Nike deciding to create a dedicated brand for all Air Jordan products. This modern remix of the Jumpman Pro throws it all the way back with an iconic court-inspired design, a touch of nostalgia, and heritage materials.

Featuring a Nike Air-Sole unit in the heel, the Air Jordan 7 Retro provides better cushioning sp wearers will get classic style with modern comfort, and the solid-rubber outsole not only reiterates the shoe's authenticity but also ensures unwavering traction on the basketball court’s smooth hardwood floor (or just the pavement in your neighborhood).

Air Jordan 7 Retro

Michael Jordan secured his second straight NBA Finals championship and even an Olympic victory with the Air Jordan 7. Meticulously designed to mirror the original Air Jordan 7 that graced his feet during historic victories, this redo lets the wearer channel the spirit of Michael Jordan's glorious era. It uses the full-length Nike Air technology to cradle your everyday step with feather-light cushioning to elevate your comfort, and the upper combines real and synthetic leather alongside textile materials and perforated detailing to enhance durability.

Our favorite part of these, however, is the retro “White Infrared” colorway that reimagines the iconic OG color scheme from the Air Jordan 6; and the molded '23' patch on the heel is a nod to Jordan's legendary jersey number.

Jordan Luka 2

As basketball shoes, the Luka 2 is built to enhance your skills like step-backs, side-steps, and quick-stop action. But with such a performance-minded design overall, we found that this is truly the most comfortable and supportive Jordan model for all kinds of athletic activities. First, the stacked midsole is extra cushioned and has a firm but still comfy underfoot support that keeps you grounded but also flexible, offering both stability and responsiveness as you shift or walk back and forth on the court. It even offers lateral support to up the energy return and side-to-side stability.

Up top, the Luka 2 also delivers support and stability with a sturdy cage that wraps around the entire upper to ensure your feet stay securely in place no matter the movement. But despite this 360-degree support design, the Luka 2 is incredibly light in weight and breathable, meaning that you can wear it both on and off the court, for a variety of sports or even workouts where cushioning, flexibility, and stability are needed.
