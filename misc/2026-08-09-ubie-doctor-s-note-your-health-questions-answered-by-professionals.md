Allergic to Aspirin and Wine? Understanding the Science of AERD and Polyps

Flushing, hives, and asthma after aspirin or wine are classic warning signs of Aspirin Exacerbated Respiratory Disease (AERD), often paired with nasal polyps and histamine or sulfite sensitivity. This reaction occurs when COX-1 inhibition triggers leukotriene-driven inflammation. Effective management includes avoiding NSAIDs and wine, taking leukotriene modifiers and corticosteroids, and considering aspirin desensitization or sinus surgery for long-term relief.
