# These Bodybuilders Recreated the Epic 'Rocky IV' Training Montage

The training montage is a sports movie staple, an essential storytelling device which signifies both the passage of time and the progress of the hero. And the Siberia-set training sequence from boxing classic Rocky IV is perhaps the most famous (and most physically extreme) of them all.

In their latest video, YouTube's Buff Dudes, aka Hudson and Brandon White, took on the Rocky IV montage workout, to see if Sylvester Stallone really was working as hard as he made it look in the movie."Usually [montages] take about three minutes," says Hudson. "Ours took five hours."

The first exercise in the montage is cutting down a tree. How much strength and stamina does it take to actually fell a tree? A surprising amount. After about five minutes, during which he has chopped through about half of the tree, Hudson is out of breath.

[Bodybuilders Recreated](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1408041067.html)

"It's probably why they do a montage to skip all the boring stuff like the rests and the heavy breathing," he says. "This is very winning; aerobic, anaerobic, really feeling it in my lats, shoulders, biceps." With a little push, he is able to get the tree down in just over 10 minutes.

The next step in the workout, demonstrated by Brandon, is chopping up all of the timber from the tree. "You know, when you watch these montages as a kid, it's super motivating, super badass," he says. "That's the highlight reel. You see the behind the scenes, reality starts setting in. [It's] a lot of hard work. You've got to take a lot of breaks, you get winded, you're tired, but it is a really good workout."

After that, they both successfully recreate the moment where Stallone carries a log across his shoulders in a crucifix position. "We didn't have snow to run through, but that was still pretty damn hard," says Brandon. They then each take it in turns to "sled dog" the other, as Stallone did with Rocky's trainer Paulie Pennino (Burt Young). It torches their quads, and Brandon concludes it's "an excellent leg workout."

They finish off Rocky IV workout with a cable pull using a truck tire slung over a rafter, which is a lot harder than either of them anticipate: "I think I've found a favorite new exercise," says Hudson.
