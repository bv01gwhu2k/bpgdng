Chemical Peels Q&A

Chemical peels are ideal for adults looking to improve skin tone, texture, and overall clarity, including concerns like acne, discoloration, and dullness. A consultation with a provider can determine the best peel type based on your skin and goals.

Results can vary depending on the type of peel and individual skin conditions. Generally, improvements can last from several months to a year. Maintenance treatments are recommended to sustain results.

Downtime varies depending on the strength of the peel. Light peels may have little to no downtime, while deeper peels can involve peeling, redness, and sensitivity for several days.
