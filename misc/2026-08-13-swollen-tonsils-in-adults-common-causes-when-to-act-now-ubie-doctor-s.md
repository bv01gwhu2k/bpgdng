Swollen tonsils in adults are most often caused by infections, including viral or bacterial tonsillitis (such as strep throat), chronic tonsillitis, and mononucleosis. Other causes include unilateral tonsillar enlargement, tonsil stones, allergies, acid reflux, and, in rare cases, tumors. Symptoms may include sore throat, difficulty swallowing, fever, and visible redness or white patches.
