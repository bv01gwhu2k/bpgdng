Explanation

A weight gain calculator is a tool that helps you understand how much weight you might gain over time. It uses your current weight, height, age, and activity level to give you an idea of how much you need to eat to gain weight.

How It Works:

Current Weight and Height: These are used to calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI), which helps determine if your current weight is in a healthy range.

Activity Level: This tells you how many calories you burn in a day. The more active you are, the more calories you need to gain weight.

Goal Setting: You can set weight goals based on how much you want to gain and how quickly you want to achieve it.

Using a weight gain calculator can be a helpful way to track your progress and make sure you are eating enough to meet your goals. It is important to remember that gaining weight should be done in a healthy way, focusing on nutritious foods and regular exercise.
