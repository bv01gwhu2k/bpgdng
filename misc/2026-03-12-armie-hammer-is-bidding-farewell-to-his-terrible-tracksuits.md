# Armie Hammer Is Bidding Farewell to His Terrible Tracksuits

Not since have we experienced a loss that cut this deeply.

"I would like to take this moment on the show to officially announce my retirement from tracksuits," Armie Hammer said during an interview with Conan O'Brien on Tuesday.

Allow us to explain. Forced to listen to people compliment his film for a grueling 14 months straight, precious being Armie Hammer decided enough was enough during the Call Me By Your Name press tour.

The result? A line-up of track suits so equal-parts terrible and praise-worthy, they might as well count as Armie Hammer's IMDB page.

[Farewell to His Terrible](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/)

"I woke up in the morning and I thought, 'You know what? I now have senioritis'—like a senior in school who has to go to class, but they hate their professors, so they're like, 'I'm going to show up in pajamas,'" the actor said. "My equivalency was tracksuits. I thought, 'You know what? If I'm going to go jump through these hoops and I'm going to continue to talk about this movie that I've talked about ad nauseum, I'm going to be comfortable.'"

[Is Bidding Farewell to](https://benpepsi.com/blog/0223080655.html)

Say what you will about the style, but damn, those things do look comfortable.

Normally, we'd recommend most dudes opt for the classic suit in a formal wear setting (and here's how to look damn good doing it). But most dudes are also not Golden Globe-nominated actor Armie Hammer, and therefore cannot pull off the Adidas stripes so effortlessly.

In the end, Hammer's parade of velvety two-pieces may be gone, but they will never be forgotten. Seriously. The actor has like 70 of them just hanging around somewhere, he says, sent custom-made from top designers like Armani, Fila, Hugo Boss, and Tom Ford.

"It got a lot more traction than I was really expecting it to," he said. "I just thought I'd be comfortable. And then it became a thing. If you get into something, they'll send it to you. For free!"
