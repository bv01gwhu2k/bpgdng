Internal Medicine

Adrenal insufficiency is a condition in which the adrenal glands do not produce enough essential hormones, leading to a range of noticeable symptoms. Common signs include persistent fatigue, muscle weakness, unexplained weight loss, low blood pressure (which may cause dizziness), and hyperpigmentation, or darkening of the skin. Because these symptoms overlap with many other conditions, they are often overlooked or misdiagnosed.
