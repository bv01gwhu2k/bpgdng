When should REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) be considered a medical emergency?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) is a sleep condition in which a person physically acts out vivid dreams, sometimes violently. It can become a medical emergency when episodes cause injury to the sleeper or a bed partner, or when severe symptoms appear suddenly. Immediate medical attention is warranted if someone punches, kicks, jumps from bed, or sustains any injury while dreaming.

Because RBD can also be an early warning sign of neurological conditions like Parkinson's disease, understanding your symptoms early matters.
