The peacoat was originally born out of utilitarian necessity—protecting Dutch and British sailors from the harsh elements of navy life in the 1800s. Back then, a men's peacoat was considered groundbreaking in its form-fitting silhouette with flared hips and thick wool blend; it was esteemed as a rugged seafaring boon that made warmth, comfort, and ease of movement possible even in the elements. A peacoat was thus a necessity to a life at sea as much as it is a necessity to any stylish guy's closet now.
