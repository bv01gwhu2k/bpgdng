Persistent fatigue is often linked to underlying conditions such as depression, neurological disorders, or chronic kidney disease. Each of these affects the body in unique ways, resulting in ongoing tiredness that rest alone cannot resolve.

Depression disrupts brain chemistry and sleep patterns, draining both mental and physical energy.

Neurological issues can interfere with nerve signaling, causing exhaustion even after minimal activity.

Chronic kidney disease allows toxins to build up in the bloodstream, leading to persistent weakness.
